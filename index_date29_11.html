How it works

One handle in. A full content report out.

CRS quietly reads your public videos — titles, descriptions, posting cadence — and turns them into a strategic snapshot: identity, momentum, and realistic next moves.

Step 01

Drop your YouTube handle

We fetch your recent uploads. No passwords. No private data. Just the content you already published.

Step 02

CRS scores your creator identity

We grade niche clarity, content patterns, timing, and (optionally) brand-fit — then compress it into one score.

Step 03

You get a next-steps playbook

Next video ideas, a short-form script blueprint, upload windows, and which sponsorships to actually accept.
